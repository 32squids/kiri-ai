KIRI AI: THE DUEL — RULESET
(Compiled from original description + clarifying Q&A)

===========================================================================
GOAL
===========================================================================
Two players duel as samurai. A player loses the moment they have been
successfully struck twice.

===========================================================================
BOARD & SETUP
===========================================================================
- The board is a line of 5 spaces, numbered 1 through 5.
- Player One starts at space 1. Player Two starts at space 5.
- Both players start in HEAVEN stance.
- Each player is always in exactly one of two stances: HEAVEN or EARTH.
- "Forward" is fixed per player for the whole match based on starting
  side: Player One's forward = toward higher-numbered spaces (toward 5).
  Player Two's forward = toward lower-numbered spaces (toward 1).
- Players may occupy the same space as their opponent.
- A player may never end movement behind the opponent (i.e. past their
  position, in the mover's forward direction), nor pass over/through the
  opponent's space along the way. Landing exactly on the opponent's
  space (a tie) is allowed.
- Spaces 1 and 5 are hard walls — you cannot move past them.

===========================================================================
HAND / CARDS
===========================================================================
Each player has a fixed set of 6 physical cards for the entire game:
  1. Forward / Backward card — one physical card with two ends. The
     player commits to which end (Forward or Backward) is in play at the
     moment they select this card for a turn — the choice is locked in
     before reveal.
  2. Stance Change / Charge card: one physical card, choice
     of end locked in at selection time.
  3. Heaven Strike (standard attack)
  4. Earth Strike (standard attack)
  5. Neutral Strike (standard attack)
  6. One Special Attack — see SPECIAL ATTACKS below.

Because the Forward/Backward pair is one physical card, and the
StanceChange/Charge pair is another, a player can never select both
members of the same pair in a single turn (e.g. can't play both Forward
and Backward in the same turn).

===========================================================================
MOVEMENT ACTIONS
===========================================================================
- Forward: move 1 space in your forward direction.
- Backward: move 1 space away from your forward direction.
- Charge: move 2 spaces in your forward direction.
- Stance Change: swap HEAVEN <-> EARTH.

Legality of movement (behind-opponent rule, wall rule, pass-through rule
for Charge) is not an action-selection restriction — illegal movement
cards CAN be selected, they simply result in no movement at all (a
no-op) when resolved. This is different from stance-gated attacks (see
below), which cannot be selected at all if the stance requirement isn't
met.

Movement cards have no cooldown, unlike attacks, see below.

FULLY EXECUTED CHARGE IS ILLEGAL: Consider Player A at position 2 and
Player B at position 3. Card 1 reveals Player A charges and Player B
moves backward. Rather than a no-op, Player A charges the open distance of
1 space to position 3, then Player B moves backward to position 4.

SIMULTANEOUS MOVEMENT CROSSING: If two simultaneous moves (same
movement type in the same reveal) would result in the players crossing/
swapping sides relative to each other, both moves are no-op'd instead.
For example, Player A is in position 2 and Player B is in position 3.
Both players reveal Card 1 to be Forward. This execution would result in
Player A and Player B swapping positions - an illegal board state - and
would result in a no-op (note the same result would occur if both players
played Charge). However, if players occupy the same space (for example,
both in position 2) and one player reveals Forward and the other reveals
Backward, they move together to the new space.

===========================================================================
STANDARD ATTACKS
===========================================================================
Each attack threatens a specific set of target space(s), always
measured in the attacker's forward direction from the attacker's own
current position:
  - Neutral Strike: hits the attacker's own space (offset 0). Usable in
    either stance.
  - Earth Strike: hits 1 space ahead (offset 1). Usable only in EARTH
    stance.
  - Heaven Strike: hits exactly 2 spaces ahead (offset 2). Usable only
    in HEAVEN stance.

If a target offset would fall outside the 1-5 board, an attack reaching
there always misses.

===========================================================================
HIT RESOLUTION (GENERAL RULE)
===========================================================================
An attack has a set of target squares (see offsets above / special
attacks below). Given an attacker and a defender:

  The attacker's strike is SUCCESSFUL if and only if:
    (a) the defender's current position is one of the attacker's target
        squares, AND
    (b) EITHER the defender did not attack this same reveal, OR the
	defender's attack is not targeting the attacker's current position

In plain terms: if both players attack in the same reveal and each
attack's targets include the other player's square, both attacks
cancel out and neither player is hit. An example: Player A at space 1
in Heaven stance plays Heaven Strike (targets space 3). Player B at space
3 in Earth stance plays Earth Strike (targets space 4). B is at A's target
(3), and A is not at B's target (4) — so A lands a successful hit. Had B
instead been in Heaven stance playing Heaven Strike (targeting space 3, A's
square), both attacks would hit and cancel.

An attack played as Card 2 is checked as legal against the player's
stance AFTER Card 1 is revealed. A Heaven Strike is legal as Card 2 when
the player starts the turn in Earth Stance only if Card 1 is Stance Change.

Note that it is impossible for both players to successfully hit each
other in the same exchange. This also prevents a tie from occurring.

===========================================================================
SPECIAL ATTACKS
===========================================================================
There is a separate pool of 3 special attacks. At the start of the game,
each player is blindly and randomly dealt one (a player knows their own
special, but not their opponent's until played). The third card is set
aside face down and never enters play. Once a player's special attack is
played, it is discarded from the game PERMANENTLY — there is no
cooldown/return, unlike standard attacks.

The three special attacks:
  1. Counterattack — usable in either stance. If the opponent's attack
     this reveal would have successfully hit you, then instead they are
     hit. If their attack would not have hit you, Counterattack is discarded
     with no effect.
  2. Zan-tetsu Strike — usable only in EARTH stance. Hits BOTH 2 spaces
     ahead AND 3 spaces ahead. After playing it, regardless of success,
     the player swaps into HEAVEN stance.
  3. Kesa Strike — usable only in HEAVEN stance. Hits the attacker's own
     space AND 1 space ahead. After playing it, regardless of success,
     the player swaps into EARTH stance.

===========================================================================
STANDARD ATTACK COOLDOWN
===========================================================================
When a standard attack (Heaven/Earth/Neutral Strike) is played, it goes
to a 1-turn discard pile: the player cannot play that same attack again
on their very next turn. After sitting out that one turn, it returns to
the hand as normal. This cooldown does not apply to movement cards.

===========================================================================
TURN STRUCTURE
===========================================================================
Each turn, both players simultaneously and privately select 2 cards from
their hand of 6 to play that turn. A player may choose which of their
two selected cards is revealed/resolved first vs. second, but decisions
are locked in prior to any cards being revealed.

Card 1 from both players is revealed at the same time and resolved.
Then Card 2 from both players is revealed at the same time and
resolved (unless the game already ended after Card 1 — see GAME END
below).

RESOLUTION ORDER WITHIN A SINGLE REVEAL:
Movement always resolves before attacks. Among movement types, the
priority order is:
   1. Charge
   2. Forward / Backward
   3. Stance Change
   4. Attacks — resolved simultaneously between both players,
      order does not matter between the two players' attacks.

Some examples:
  - If one player's card this reveal is a movement and the other's is
    an attack: the mover's movement fully resolves first (against the
    opponent's pre-move position, for legality purposes), and then the
    attack is checked against the opponent's NEW (post-move) position.
  - If both players' cards this reveal are movement types: they resolve
    according to the priority order above (e.g. a Charge resolves
    before a Forward/Backward played by the other player that same
    reveal). If both players play the SAME movement type in the same
    reveal (e.g. both Charge), they resolve simultaneously, checked
    against each other's pre- and post-step positions (see SIMULTANEOUS
    MOVEMENT CROSSING).
  - If both players' cards this reveal are attacks: resolved
    simultaneously, per the general hit-resolution rule.

===========================================================================
GAME END
===========================================================================
- When a player is successfully hit, they record 1 hit. In the paper game,
  it's recorded as flipping their character over.
- Hit checks happen immediately after EACH card reveal (not just once
  at the end of the turn).
- If a player reaches 2 total hits, the game ends immediately.
